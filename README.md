# KnubPad I1
Had you ever wanted a SayoDevice for your shenanigans in OSU or video editing or animation, but you're broke as fish? Well, introducing the new KnubPad I1! A scuffed but cheap version of your SayoDevice. No screen, cheapest components available, and a price that'll make your wallet cry tears of joy. (OR AT LEAST I HOPE!)
# Idea
This is my first hardware project, not admitting anything else here. There will be mistakes made when making this project, and I will probably take forever to finish this, but I just want a keypad that can just work with no compromises. Well, some compromises to keep costs down, but mostly just something that functions well to my liking.
